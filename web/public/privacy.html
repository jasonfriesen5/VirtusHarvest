VIRTUSHARVEST

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

Virtus Harvest is a companion app for the Virtus grain cart scale, published by Jason Friesen Janzen. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

The short version. We collect only what the app needs to record your harvest and sync it between your own devices. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and there is no analytics or tracking software in the app. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Email addressUsed to create your account and sign you in.
NamesThe operator and driver names you enter yourself, so loads can be attributed to the person who hauled them.
LocationA GPS point recorded with each load, and the field boundaries you draw. Collected only while the app is in use — never in the background.
Harvest recordsWeights, crop, moisture, and the farm, field, truck, destination and season names you create, plus any notes you add.
Scale informationSerial number, firmware version and battery level read from your own scale over Bluetooth.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifier, and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No contacts, photos, health, financial or payment information
  • No camera or photo access at all — the app never asks for either

Why we collect it

Solely to make the app work: to record your loads, show yields by field, keep your records synchronised across your devices, and connect to your scale. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the app.

Where it is stored

Records are saved on your device first, so the app keeps working without a signal, and are synchronised to your account when you are back in service. Cloud storage is provided by Supabase. Your data is protected by row-level security rules so that only your account can read or write it.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Google Maps — draws the map when you view or draw field boundaries.

These are service providers acting on our behalf. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and no third party receives it for their own purposes.

Deleting your data

Open More ▸ Settings ▸ Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and every record attached to it — loads, farms, fields, trucks, destinations, operators, seasons and boundaries — and clears the copies held on that device. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

To delete individual records instead, remove them in the app; deletions sync to your other devices.

How long we keep it

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists, because harvest history is the point of the app. Deleting your account removes them.

Children

Virtus Harvest is a tool for agricultural work and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about your data, or a request relating to it: support@virtusharvest.com
