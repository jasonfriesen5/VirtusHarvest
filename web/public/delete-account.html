Delete Your Account

Last updated 28 August 2026

Virtus Harvest is a companion app for the Virtus grain cart scale, published by Jason Friesen Janzen. This page explains how to delete your account and everything stored with it.

The short version. Open the app, go to More › Settings › Delete Account, and confirm. Your account and all of your harvest data are removed immediately and permanently. If you no longer have the app installed, email us and we will do it for you.

Deleting from inside the app

  1. Open Virtus Harvest and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the More tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Scroll to the Account section at the bottom.
  5. Next to Delete Account, tap Delete.
  6. Confirm when asked. The deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app

Email support@virtusharvest.com from the email address the account was registered with, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm the request and complete it within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address so nobody else can delete your account.

Deleting individual records

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove data. Everything you create in the app can be deleted on its own, and the deletion is applied to our servers as well as to your device:

  • Loads — open a recorded load and delete it.
  • Farms and fields — from the Fields tab, open the item and delete it. Deleting a field also removes the boundary drawn for it.
  • Trucks and destinations — from their tabs.
  • Grain carts — from the Device tab.
  • Operators and seasons — from the More tab.

If you delete something while offline, the app records the deletion and applies it the next time it syncs, so it is not silently undone when you come back into coverage.

If you would rather we removed particular records for you, email support@virtusharvest.com and tell us what to delete. We will complete the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Everything below is removed permanently, at the same time as the account itself:

AccountYour sign-in record and email address.
WeighingsEvery recorded load, including its weight, crop, moisture and the location it was recorded at.
Farms and fieldsField names, areas and the boundaries you drew on the map.
TrucksTruck names, plates, drivers and capacities.
DestinationsDelivery destinations you created.
OperatorsOperator names and roles.
Grain cartsCart names and the scale serial numbers paired to them.
Seasons and cropsSeason records and your crop list.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing of yours is kept after deletion. There is no retention period, no archive copy and no soft-delete: the records are removed from the database rather than flagged as hidden.

Two things are worth knowing:

  • Data on your own device. Anything saved locally on your phone or tablet is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Deleting your account does not reach onto a device you are no longer signed in on.
  • Ordinary server logs. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs for a short period as part of running the service. These are not tied to your harvest records and are discarded on their normal schedule.

Deleting the app without deleting your account

Uninstalling Virtus Harvest does not delete your account. If you want your data gone, use one of the two routes above before or after uninstalling.
