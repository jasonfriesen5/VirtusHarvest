## Description  *(1,575 / 4,000)*

```
Virtus Harvest turns your grain cart into a complete harvest record.

Pair the app with your Virtus scale over Bluetooth and every load is weighed, logged and totalled as you go — no notebooks, no whiteboard in the shop, no adding it up at the end of the day.

LIVE WEIGHING
• Large, high-contrast weight display readable from the tractor seat
• Tare and zero from the app
• Stable-weight indicator so you know when a reading has settled
• Kilograms, pounds or bushels

AUTOMATIC UNLOAD DETECTION
• The app notices when grain comes off the cart and logs the load for you
• Adjustable drop trigger so it suits your cart and your pace
• Manual unload whenever you prefer to do it yourself

ORGANISED THE WAY YOU FARM
• Farms, fields and field boundaries on a map
• Trucks with capacities and a live load bar
• Destinations, operators and seasons
• Crop and moisture per load, with dry-weight calculation
• GPS location recorded with every load

CROP SAFEGUARDS
• Warns before you unload a crop onto a truck already carrying a different one
• Flags when a load's crop doesn't match the field it came from

WORKS WHERE YOU WORK
• Everything is stored on the device first, so a dead zone doesn't stop you
• Syncs to your account when you're back in service
• iPhone and iPad, portrait or landscape
• Full English and Spanish

SCALE MAINTENANCE BUILT IN
• Calibration tool with certified-weight entry
• Battery, voltage and sensitivity readouts
• Firmware updates installed over Bluetooth, entirely inside the app

Virtus Harvest requires a Virtus grain cart scale to read weights.
```

No spaces after commas — spaces count against the limit.

Note: `harvest` is arguably wasted, since it already appears in the app name and
Apple indexes that separately. Swapping it for a term you don't otherwise rank
for (e.g. `grower`, `acres`) would buy ~8 characters of reach.

## Blockers that live *outside* the version page

These are the ones that keep "Add for Review" greyed out with no obvious cause:

- **App Information** → Age Rating, Content Rights, Primary Category, Copyright
- **Pricing and Availability** → price tier and country availability
- **App Privacy** → must be **Published**, not merely saved
